Customer operations for a retail bank

One platform, three users. Customers get an assistant that answers policy questions and files complaints. Compliance staff work those complaints in the Case Desk. Analysts ask questions across Northwind's cases and the public CFPB complaint data. Everything runs on Google Cloud, and falls back to a local open-source stack when Vertex AI is not available.

Customers

Customer assistant

Multi-agent ADK assistant (coordinator plus intake, policy and status agents). Also reachable through Dialogflow CX.

 Compliance and operations staff

Case Desk

Queue of complaints with urgency and regulation tags. Update status and add notes; every change is an event on Pub/Sub.

 Internal analysts

Analyst

Ask in plain English about case trends and compare them with industry complaints in BigQuery.

Northwind Bank is fictional and all cases are demo data. The internal tools are open so anyone can try them; in production they would sit behind Google sign-in (IAP). Architecture, Terraform and CI are in the GitHub repo.
