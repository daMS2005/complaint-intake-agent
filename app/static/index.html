Northwind Resolve

Complaint handling for a retail bank

Customers tell an AI assistant what went wrong. Specialists resolve it before the legal deadline. Managers see where complaints come from. Northwind Bank is fictional; the regulations and the industry data are real.

How a complaint moves through it

  1. The customer signs in and asks for help. The assistant answers policy questions, or files a case with product, urgency and the rules that apply (Reg E, Reg Z, RESPA, FCRA, FDCPA).
  2. The case lands in the Case Desk with its legal deadline. 10 business days for Reg E errors, 15 days otherwise. Urgent cases alert the compliance team immediately.
  3. A specialist works it. Internal notes, a reply to the customer, a status change. Every change is an event other systems can react to.
  4. The customer sees the outcome under My account, without calling in.
  5. Managers watch the trends. Deadline risk, and an analyst agent that compares Northwind's cases with complaints across the industry.

Built on Google Cloud (ADK agents on Gemini, Dialogflow CX, Cloud Run, Firestore, Pub/Sub, BigQuery, Identity Platform, Terraform) with a fallback to a local open-source stack. Architecture and code.
