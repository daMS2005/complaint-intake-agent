What happens to a complaint

  1. The customer signs in and describes the problem. The assistant asks what it needs and files the case, or answers from the bank's policies if there is nothing to file.
  2. The case gets its deadline: 10 business days for card and transfer errors, 15 days for everything else. Fraud and other urgent cases alert the compliance team right away.
  3. A specialist picks it up in the Case Desk, replies to the customer and records what was done.
  4. The customer sees the reply in their account. Managers see which cases are close to their deadline and how Northwind compares with complaints across the industry.
