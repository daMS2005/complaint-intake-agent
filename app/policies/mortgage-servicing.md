# Mortgage servicing (RESPA / Regulation X)

## When your loan is transferred
If your mortgage servicing is transferred to or from Northwind, you receive a notice from both servicers. For 60 days after the transfer, a payment sent on time to the old servicer cannot be treated as late, and no late fee can be charged.

## Errors and requests for information
You can send a written notice of error, for example a payment that was not credited. We acknowledge it within 5 business days and correct the error or explain why there was none within 30 business days.

## Help if you are behind (loss mitigation)
If you are struggling to pay, contact us about repayment plans, forbearance or a loan modification. We will not start a foreclosure until you are more than 120 days delinquent. If we receive a complete loss mitigation application more than 37 days before a scheduled foreclosure sale, we will not move for judgment or conduct the sale while we evaluate it.

## Foreclosure concerns
If you believe a foreclosure is being started in error, for example because payments were made, contact us immediately. These complaints are always treated as urgent.
