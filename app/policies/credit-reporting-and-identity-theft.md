# Credit reporting and identity theft (FCRA)

## Disputing information we report
Northwind reports account information to the credit bureaus. If you believe something we reported is inaccurate, you can dispute it with us directly or with the credit bureau. We investigate within 30 days and correct the information with every bureau we reported it to.

## Identity theft
If someone opened an account in your name, tell us right away. We close the account, mark it as fraud with the credit bureaus, and stop collection. We may ask for an identity theft report from IdentityTheft.gov. You can also place a free fraud alert or a security freeze with the credit bureaus.
