# Checking and savings account fees

## Overdraft fees
Northwind charges a $35 overdraft fee when we pay a transaction that overdraws your checking account, up to 3 fees per day. We do not charge an overdraft fee if your account is overdrawn by $5 or less at the end of the day, or for transactions under $5. Debit card and ATM overdrafts are only paid if you opted in to overdraft coverage (Regulation E opt-in).

## Overdraft fee refunds
If you were charged an overdraft fee you believe is wrong, for example because a deposit was posted late or your available balance was positive, contact us within 60 days. We review the posting order and refund fees caused by our error. We also refund one overdraft fee per 12 months as a courtesy, on request.

## Non-sufficient funds (NSF)
Northwind does not charge NSF (returned item) fees.

## Monthly maintenance fee
Everyday Checking has a $12 monthly fee, waived with $500 in monthly direct deposits or a $1,500 minimum daily balance. Savings accounts have no monthly fee.
