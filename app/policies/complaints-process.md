# How Northwind handles complaints

## Filing a complaint
You can file a complaint by chat, phone, branch or mail. Each complaint gets a case ID in the form C-XXXXXXXX.

## Response times
Urgent complaints (fraud, money at risk now, frozen funds, imminent foreclosure or repossession) are escalated to a specialist who contacts you within one business day. All other complaints receive a response within 15 days, and a final answer within 60 days.

## If you are not satisfied
You can ask for your case to be reviewed by our Customer Advocacy Office, or submit a complaint to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau at consumerfinance.gov/complaint.
