# Credit card billing disputes (Regulation Z, Fair Credit Billing Act)

## What you can dispute
Charges you did not make, charges for goods or services you did not receive or that were not as agreed, math errors, and payments not credited.

## How to dispute
Contact us within 60 days after the statement with the error was sent. Tell us your name, the card (last four digits only), the amount, and why you believe it is wrong. You do not have to pay the disputed amount while we investigate, and we will not report it as delinquent.

## Our timeline
We acknowledge your dispute within 30 days and resolve it within two billing cycles, and never more than 90 days.

## Unauthorized use
By law your liability for unauthorized use of a credit card is capped at $50. Northwind's Zero Liability policy means you pay $0 for unauthorized charges reported promptly.
