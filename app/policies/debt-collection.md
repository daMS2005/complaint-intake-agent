# Collecting past-due balances (FDCPA practices)

## How we contact you
Northwind and the agencies we use follow Fair Debt Collection Practices Act standards. We do not call before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. your local time, and we stop calling you at work if you tell us your employer does not allow it.

## Disputing a debt
If you believe you do not owe a debt, for example because it was already paid, tell us in writing within 30 days of our first notice. We pause collection until we send you verification.

## Paid accounts
Once a balance is paid we notify any collection agency within 5 business days and update the credit bureaus.
