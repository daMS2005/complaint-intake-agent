# Unauthorized debit card and electronic transfers (Regulation E)

## Reporting unauthorized transactions
Tell us right away if your debit card is lost or stolen or you see transactions you did not make. Call us, use the app, or chat with us. We will block the card and issue a new one.

## Your liability
If you tell us within 2 business days after learning of the loss or theft, you can lose no more than $50. If you tell us after 2 business days, you could lose up to $500. If an unauthorized transfer appears on your statement and you do not tell us within 60 days after the statement was sent, you may not get back money lost after those 60 days. Northwind's Zero Liability promise covers debit card purchases reported promptly, so in practice most customers pay nothing.

## How we investigate
We will investigate and tell you the result within 10 business days. If we need more time, we may take up to 45 days (90 days for new accounts, point-of-sale or foreign transactions); in that case we give you provisional credit within 10 business days so you have use of the money while we investigate. If we find no error, we explain why in writing and you may ask for the documents we relied on.
